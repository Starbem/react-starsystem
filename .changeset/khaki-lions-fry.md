Add Popover component (ID-3185) built on @radix-ui/react-popover, with configurable `trigger`, `content` (slot), `side`, `align`, and `onOpenChange`. Supports rich content (forms, lists, etc.), automatic focus trap, and closes on Escape or an outside click.

New runtime dependency: `@radix-ui/react-popover`.
